## 🧭 First-Run Configuration Guide

When you open the application for the first time, a few decisions shape your experience.

### Choosing Your Vault Location

Your vault is where your notes live. Pick a folder that syncs with whatever backup strategy you already trust. Many users keep it inside a documents directory or a dedicated drive letter reserved for knowledge work.

### Selecting a Theme

Themes in Logseq-2026 range from minimal light palettes to deep, low-contrast dark modes. Pick one that reduces eye strain during long sessions. You can change it later without losing a single note.

### Setting Your Preferred Language

The multilingual interface can be adjusted at any time. Switching languages does not alter your content — it only changes the labels and menus you interact with.

### Enabling the Graph View

The graph view is optional but transformative. Enable it once your vault has more than a handful of pages, and watch your ideas arrange themselves into clusters you never consciously planned.

### Configuring Shortcuts

Keyboard shortcuts turn a good outliner into a fast one. Take ten minutes to review the default keymap and adjust it to your muscle memory.

## 🧱 Architectural Philosophy

Understanding why a tool behaves the way it does helps you use it well. Logseq-2026 rests on a few core beliefs.

### Local-First

Your notes are plain files on your own disk. This means you can back them up, move them, and read them with any text editor. It also means you are not dependent on a remote service being online to access your own thinking.

### Block-Oriented

Everything is a block. A paragraph, a bullet, a task, a quote — all are blocks that can be referenced, embedded, and rearranged. This granularity is what makes the system flexible.

### Graph-Native

Links are first-class citizens. The graph is not a gimmick; it is the primary way your knowledge organizes itself over time.

### Extensible

Themes, plugins, and custom workflows keep the tool from becoming rigid. You can shape the environment to match how your mind works rather than the other way around.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Situations

Even a well-designed tool occasionally hits a snag. Here are the most common situations and how to approach them.

### The Application Does Not Start

Check that your Windows build is current. If a recent Windows update is pending, install it and try again. Also verify that the package completed its save process without interruption.

### The Graph View Feels Slow

Very large vaults can make the graph view heavy. Consider filtering the graph to show only recently modified pages, or reduce the number of nodes displayed at once.

### Notes Seem Out of Sync

Because storage is local-first, syncing depends on how you have configured your vault folder. If you use a cloud drive, ensure it has finished uploading before switching devices.

### A Shortcut Is Not Responding

Review the keymap in preferences. If a conflict exists with another application, reassign the shortcut to something unique.

### Language Does Not Change

Confirm the change in preferences and restart the application once. Some interface strings are cached until the next launch.

For anything not covered here, remember that 24/7 customer support is part of the experience. You are not expected to solve every puzzle alone.
